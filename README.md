# AirRenda Public Documentation

AirRenda is an address-level Airbnb market analytics tool for property investors, buyers, agents, property managers, and acquisition teams. Enter an address or click a point on the map and get a first-pass report: the AirRenda Score (0–100), nearby listing activity, nightly-rate context, competition, six investment risk signals, and an estimated revenue range.

AirRenda screens properties you are already considering. It does not list homes for sale, it does not manage live Airbnb pricing, and it does not replace underwriting, legal review, or a financial model. The question it answers is earlier: does this address deserve a viewing, a model, an offer, or a pass?

A higher score is not automatically a better buy. Scores of 41–65 are the usual investment sweet spot. Scores above 80 often mean heavy supply and pricing pressure.

## At a glance

- Browse an interactive Airbnb market heatmap on the free Explorer plan. No account and no credit card are required for the heatmap.
- Click anywhere on the globe, or type an address with autocomplete, to analyze a specific location.
- Receive a full report in seconds for most locations. Each new address uses one report from the plan balance.
- Read the AirRenda Score, its four-factor breakdown, and a market category from Unproven through Saturated.
- See nearby active listings, nightly-rate ranges, the interquartile price band (P25–P75), property-type mix, ratings, Superhost share, guest-favorite share, and discount pressure.
- Review six investment risk signals: Demand Strength, Revenue Quality, Competition Intensity, Operational Complexity, Regulation Exposure, and Pricing Pressure.
- Use the estimated monthly revenue range as a screening input. Listed nightly rates are asking prices of nearby listings. The occupancy figure is an estimate from local listing density, not measured bookings.
- Open top nearby competitor listings and a map of listing density around the address.
- Download a PDF. Saved reports stay in the dashboard and can be filtered by city and country.
- Refresh a saved address once per calendar month on a paid plan without using another report.
- Use Check Prices Around to browse live nearby listings on the map. That search uses one report from the balance.
- Research addresses worldwide. The product interface is available in 12 languages.
- Compare a shortlist with the same report structure before spending time on viewings or a full model.

## What a report is, and what it is not

Every paid plan includes the same full report. Plans differ by how many new addresses you can analyze per month, not by which analytics are locked.

The report describes the short-term rental market around an address at the time it is generated. It does not inspect the unit, confirm that short-term rental use is legal, or forecast what that specific property will earn. Thin markets produce thinner evidence, and that usually shows up as a lower score and an Unproven or Early Opportunity category.

Nightly prices in the report are listed rates on nearby listings. They are not confirmed booked-night ADR. Occupancy is a density-based estimate, not a count of actual guest nights. Treat the revenue range as a scenario input, then add your own costs, taxes, fees, financing, seasonality, and ramp-up.

## Plans in brief

Public plans are Explorer (free heatmap), Buyer, Investor, and Agency, plus a custom Enterprise option. Subscribers can add pay-as-you-go report packs. Current prices, allowances, and billing rules are on the live pricing page.
