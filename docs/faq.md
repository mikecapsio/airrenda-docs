# AirRenda FAQ

Short answers to the questions investors, buyers, and agents actually ask. The live FAQ is https://airrenda.com/en/faq. Pricing details are https://airrenda.com/en/pricing. Legal terms are https://airrenda.com/en/tos and https://airrenda.com/en/privacy-policy.

## Product

### What is AirRenda?

AirRenda.com is an Airbnb market analytics tool for people evaluating a property. You enter an address or click the map and get a report: AirRenda Score (0–100), nearby listing count, nightly-rate ranges, property-type mix, six investment risk signals, an estimated revenue range, top nearby listings, a map, and a PDF.

It is not a listing marketplace and it does not sell property. It answers an earlier question: does this address deserve more time?

### Who is it for?

Short-term rental investors, buyers who might depend on Airbnb income, real estate agents fielding that question, property managers qualifying owner leads, and acquisition analysts ranking a pipeline. The same report serves all of them. Volume is what changes between plans.

Guides by role are in the `for/` folder of these docs and at https://airrenda.com/en/for.

### How does it work?

1. Choose a location on the globe or with address autocomplete.
2. AirRenda reviews nearby short-term rental listings: counts, prices, property types, ratings, reviews, host signals, and discounting.
3. You get a scored report, saved to the dashboard.

Most reports finish in under 10 seconds.

### Does it work worldwide?

Yes, wherever short-term rental listing data exists. Large cities and resort markets usually have richer comps. Smaller markets often have fewer listings, which should show up as a lower score and an Unproven or Early Opportunity category. That is a result, not a missing country.

The interface is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, and Vietnamese.

### Does it work for apartments and houses?

Yes. Apartments, houses, condos, villas, studios, townhomes, and similar types all run as an address. The report does not inspect the unit. It describes the market around it.

### Can I click the map instead of typing an address?

Yes. Click a point, or type an address. Both end in the same kind of report.

### How long does a report take?

Usually under 10 seconds. Some locations take longer.

### Is there an example?

Yes. https://airrenda.com/en/example-report

## The score

### What is the AirRenda Score?

A 0–100 composite of demand (30%), revenue potential (25%), competition balance (25%), and market quality (20%). Full bands and how to use them: [How the AirRenda Score works](how-the-airrenda-score-works.md).

### What is a good score?

For most new investors, **41–65** (Strong Opportunity): demand looks real, and competition may still be manageable.

### Is a higher score always better?

No. **66–80** is a competitive market. **Above 80** usually means oversupply and pricing pressure. **Below 40** means demand is early or unproven. Do not sort a shortlist by "highest score wins."

### Can the score tell me whether to buy?

No. It is a first-pass market screen. It is not a buy or sell instruction and not a substitute for a financial model.

## The report

### What is in a paid report?

- AirRenda Score, market category, and four-factor breakdown
- Active nearby listing count and a map
- Average, median, minimum, and maximum listed nightly prices, plus P25–P75
- Property-type split, including entire homes versus private rooms
- Average rating, Superhost share, guest-favorite share, discount pressure
- Six risk signals
- Estimated monthly revenue range (low / mid / high)
- Market narratives and top nearby competitor listings
- PDF download and a saved dashboard entry

Paid plans do not differ in which of these you get.

### What are the six risk signals?

Demand Strength, Revenue Quality, Competition Intensity, Operational Complexity, Regulation Exposure, and Pricing Pressure. How to read each one is in [How to read a report](how-to-read-a-report.md).

### Does a report include regulation risk?

It includes a Regulation Exposure signal based on patterns such as dense entire-home supply. Those patterns are often what later draw city rules. The signal is not legal advice and cannot tell you whether a specific unit may operate as a short-term rental. Verify permits, zoning, taxes, building rules, and HOA limits locally.

### Can I compare several properties?

Yes. Every report is saved. Filter by city or country and compare score, rates, category, and listing density with the same structure on each address.

### How accurate is it?

Reports use available market data and describe conditions at generation time. Listings come and go, seasons move prices, and platforms change what they display. No analytics product is a guarantee. Run a refresh close to an offer date and verify anything that would change the decision.

### Can I download or share a report?

Paid reports include a PDF. Share it as market research with a date on it, not as advice to buy or a promised return.

## Revenue estimates

### Can AirRenda estimate Airbnb income?

Yes, as a **range**, not a promise. You get low, mid, and high monthly figures from nearby listed prices and an estimated occupancy rate. Put your own costs, taxes, fees, and financing on top.

### Are the nightly rates booked ADR?

No. They are **listed asking prices** of nearby listings. Hosts often list above what they collect. Use the median and the P25–P75 band for a base case. Do not underwrite the maximum.

### Is occupancy measured from real bookings?

No. Occupancy in the report is an **estimate inferred from how dense the nearby listing market is**. It is not a count of guest nights, calendar fills, or payouts. Do not describe AirRenda as a historical occupancy or RevPAR database.

### Can the estimate replace an investment calculator?

No. It is the market input. A calculator still needs purchase price, loan terms, furnishing, cleaning, management, fees, taxes, vacancy you believe, and a downside case. If the deal only works on the high revenue case, treat it as fragile.

## Plans, billing, and accounts

### What does the free Explorer plan include?

Unlimited access to the interactive market heatmap, including browsing cities worldwide. No credit card. It does not include address reports, the score, rate detail, risk signals, or PDFs.

### How much does AirRenda cost?

As published in October 2026: Buyer $49/month (10 reports), Investor $139/month (30 reports), Agency $299/month (70 reports). Enterprise is custom. Confirm on https://airrenda.com/en/pricing before you rely on these numbers.

### What is the difference between Buyer, Investor, and Agency?

Report volume, plus priority support on Agency. The report itself is the same. Investor and Agency are the practical choices once you are comparing many addresses. Details: [Plans and reports](plans-and-reports.md).

### How do reports work?

One new address uses one report. Subscription reports reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. Reopening a saved report does not use another credit.

### What is a refresh?

A refresh updates a saved address with newer market data. Paid plans include one refresh per saved address each calendar month. It does not use a report. Use it before an offer or a renegotiation.

### What is Check Prices Around?

A live map search of nearby listings. It uses one report from your balance. The scored report is still the thing to read for the decision.

### What are top-up packs?

One-time bundles for subscribers who need more reports than the monthly plan includes: 5, 15, 30, 60, or 100 reports, priced on the pricing page. They require an active paid plan. They are not a second subscription. Storage and cancellation rules are defined on the pricing page and in the Terms.

### Do I need an account?

Not for the heatmap. Yes for address reports: Google sign-in, then a paid plan. You must be at least 18.

### Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel in dashboard settings or the customer portal. Access lasts until the end of the current period. There is no partial refund for unused days in that period.

### Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade in dashboard settings. The change applies immediately and the allowance adjusts.

### Do you offer refunds?

Requests are reviewed case by case and should be emailed to team@airrenda.com within 14 days of purchase. Once a report has been generated, delivery of the digital service is treated as complete. Refunds may be refused in cases of fraud, manipulation, or abuse.

### Do you sell my searches?

No. AirRenda does not sell personal data or the addresses you search. Addresses are stored so reports, the dashboard, refreshes, and comparisons can work. The Privacy Policy is the full description.

### Is payment information stored by AirRenda?

No. Card details are handled by the certified payment provider named in the Terms. AirRenda does not keep your card number.

## Limits

### Can AirRenda replace due diligence?

No. It does not provide legal, tax, financial, appraisal, lending, zoning, or inspection advice. Use it to decide which addresses earn that spend.

### Does it set my nightly prices?

No. It gives market context before you buy or take on a property. Dynamic pricing for a live listing is a different product. See [AirRenda vs PriceLabs](vs/pricelabs.md).

### Does it have a public API?

These docs do not describe a public API. Use the product and official support. Do not expect unpublished endpoints to be supported.

### Who operates the service, and how do I get help?

The Terms and Privacy Policy state that AirRenda.com is operated by BnBDetector. Contact: https://airrenda.com/en/contact and team@airrenda.com.

## Choosing a tool

### Is AirRenda an AirDNA alternative?

For fast address screening, yes. For historical occupancy, ADR, and broad market benchmarking, AirDNA is the deeper platform. Many people use AirRenda first and a data platform later. See [AirRenda vs AirDNA](vs/airdna.md).

### How is it different from a revenue calculator?

Calculators lead with one income number. AirRenda leads with whether the micro-market supports a number at all, and its own revenue output is a range with stated limits. See [AirRenda vs Rabbu](vs/rabbu.md).

### How is it different from a pricing tool?

Pricing tools help a live listing. AirRenda helps the purchase decision that happens before that listing exists. See [AirRenda vs PriceLabs](vs/pricelabs.md).
