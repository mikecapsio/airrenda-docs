# AirRenda for Airbnb Investors

Airbnb investing fails when the first assumptions are too kind. A strong city, a renovated kitchen, or a broker's peak-season screenshot can still sit on a street with weak rates, the wrong property type, or more professional hosts than you can outrun.

AirRenda is the screen you run before that story becomes a model. You put in the address. You get the nearby short-term rental market in one report: AirRenda Score, listing density, listed nightly rates, competition, six risk signals, and a revenue range you are allowed to distrust.

## The problem it is meant to solve

- Seller decks and broker notes overstate what a property will do on Airbnb.
- City averages hide a dead block inside a famous market, and the reverse.
- Full underwriting is slow. Weak deals should die before they consume a weekend.

## How investors use it

1. Run every address on the live shortlist. Same report, every time.
2. Sort by market category first. Strong Opportunity (score 41–65) goes to the front. Saturated (above 80) and Unproven (below 20) need a reason to stay.
3. Read listing count, property-type fit, the P25–P75 rate band, and Regulation Exposure before you look at the mid revenue case.
4. Only the survivors enter the spreadsheet: price, debt, furnishing, cleaning, management, taxes, and a downside case that uses the low end of the rate band.
5. Refresh the saved address close to an offer. Paid plans include one refresh per address each calendar month, and it does not use another report.

The revenue range is not your underwriting. Listed rates are asking prices. Occupancy is estimated from listing density, not from bookings. If the deal only survives the high case, it is not a deal yet.

## What investors should not expect

AirRenda will not tell you to buy. It will not confirm that nightly rental is legal. It will not price the building. A high score with a high Regulation Exposure signal is a crowded market that may also be a political target. Read both.

## Questions

**Can AirRenda tell me whether to buy?**
No. It tells you whether the nearby Airbnb market supports spending more time. Modeling, rules, taxes, financing, and the inspection are still yours.

**What should I check first?**
Listing density, whether your property type matches the mix, the middle of the rate range, competitor quality, and saturation. Then the score band.

**Is it useful if I only buy one property a year?**
Yes. The Buyer plan (10 reports a month) is enough to screen a handful of finalists and not rely on a single listing's photos. Confirm the current allowance on the pricing page.
