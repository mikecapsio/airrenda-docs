# AirRenda for Property Buyers

Buyers get attached before they check the short-term rental market. The apartment is bright, the city is on every travel list, and someone says the neighbor "does really well on Airbnb." None of that is a market.

AirRenda is the pause. Before a second viewing or an offer, you run the address and see whether nearby listings, rates, and competition make an Airbnb thesis plausible.

## The problem it is meant to solve

- The property feels right before any local numbers have been checked.
- Peak-season examples and seller stories set the revenue in your head.
- Once you are emotionally committed, weak signals get explained away.

## How buyers use it

1. Run the address before you book another trip to see it, and again before you offer if weeks have passed. Use the monthly refresh on a saved address so you are not paying twice for the same check-in.
2. Read the category in words. "Strong Opportunity" and "Saturated" are different decisions even when both sound like "a lot of Airbnbs."
3. Ask better questions with the report in hand: What do similar places actually list per night? How many professional hosts are already here? Has the building or the city restricted short stays?
4. If you are choosing between two or three homes, run all of them. A favorite that scores as Unproven is a different purchase than a favorite in a proven pocket.

You do not need an enterprise dashboard for this. You need to know whether one address deserves the rest of your diligence.

## How to read a result without scaring yourself or lying to yourself

- A low score does not mean "never buy." It means you should not pay an Airbnb premium for unproven demand. Buy it as a home, or as a long-term rental, if those numbers work.
- A high score does not mean "safe income." Above 80, assume you will compete hard and that discounting may already be common.
- The revenue range is a sketch of the surrounding market. It is not what your unit will deposit. Listed rates are asking prices. Occupancy is an estimate, not a history of filled nights.

Walk through the sections in [How to read a report](../how-to-read-a-report.md).

## What still sits outside the report

Local legality, building rules, HOA fines, taxes, furnishing, cleaning, and the mortgage. AirRenda does not replace those. It tells you whether they are worth starting.

If an agent is helping you, ask them to run the address with you rather than answer "it'll do great in summer" from memory. The agent-side guide is [For real estate agents](real-estate-agents.md).

## Questions

**Can AirRenda guarantee Airbnb income?**
No. Performance depends on your pricing, occupancy, rules, design, operations, expenses, and the property itself.

**When should I run it?**
Before an offer, and before you spend a serious block of time underwriting. The point is to catch a weak market while walking away is still cheap.

**What if the score is low and I still love the place?**
Keep the place in the running for non-Airbnb reasons. Drop or heavily discount any offer that only works if the Airbnb case is true.
