# Plans and Reports

AirRenda charges for address reports, not for a locked feature tier. Buyer, Investor, and Agency all receive the full report: score, four-factor breakdown, six risk signals, rate context, revenue range, comps, map, PDF, and dashboard history. The free Explorer plan is the heatmap only.

Prices and pack sizes change. Before you buy or quote a price, use https://airrenda.com/en/pricing. The Terms of Service at https://airrenda.com/en/tos control billing disputes. Figures below match the public pricing page as of October 2026.

You can upgrade or downgrade from dashboard settings. Plan changes take effect immediately and the report allowance is adjusted to the new plan. Subscriptions renew each cycle until you cancel.

## How a report is counted

- Generating a report for a **new address** uses one report from your balance.
- Opening a report you already saved does not use another report.
- **Refreshing** a saved address is included once per calendar month on paid plans and does not use a report. Use it when the same property is still on your shortlist and you want current market data.
- **Check Prices Around**, the live map search of nearby listings, uses one report each time you run it.
- Unused reports on a subscription **do not roll over**. The monthly allowance resets on the billing-cycle date.
- Cancel anytime from dashboard settings or the customer portal. You keep the plan until the period ends. Unused time in that period is not partially refunded.

If you are unsure which action spends a credit, assume a brand-new address and a live "prices around" search do, and that reopening a saved report does not.

## What every paid report includes

- AirRenda Score (0–100), market category, and four-factor breakdown
- Six investment risk signals
- Active listing count
- Average, median, minimum, and maximum listed nightly prices, plus the P25–P75 band
- Estimated monthly revenue range (low / mid / high), with occupancy shown as an estimate
- Entire-home versus private-room mix, ratings, Superhost share, guest-favorite share, and discount pressure
- Market narratives, top nearby listings, and a map of listing density
- PDF download
- Dashboard history with city and country filters
- One monthly refresh per saved address

Agency adds priority support. Enterprise adds custom volume, a dedicated account manager, team onboarding, invoicing, and priority support with an SLA, as described on the pricing page.

## Accounts

The heatmap can be used without an account. Address reports require a free Google sign-in plus a paid plan. One account's report balance is what the dashboard shows; do not expect a shared team seat structure unless you are on an Enterprise arrangement you have actually agreed.

## Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. Email team@airrenda.com within 14 days of purchase with the account email and purchase details. Because the product is digital, once a report has been generated, delivery is treated as complete. Refunds can be refused where there is fraud, manipulation, or abuse. Top-up purchases are subject to the same Terms.

## Which plan to start with

- One property, or two or three you already love: Buyer is enough to screen them and leave room for a refresh cycle later, as long as you stay inside 10 new addresses.
- A real search across neighborhoods: Investor. Thirty reports is the difference between sampling a city and actually ranking a list.
- Client work or a weekly pipeline: Agency, or Enterprise if you need invoicing and a named contact.

If you only want to wander a map, stay on Explorer until an address is worth a report.
