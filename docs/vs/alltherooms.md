# AirRenda vs AllTheRooms

AllTheRooms is associated with broad short-term rental intelligence for teams that want market-wide data. AirRenda is for people who do not need a data warehouse to decide whether one property deserves a closer look.

If you are an individual investor, a buyer's agent, or a small acquisitions desk, the useful question is often simple: is this address strong enough to underwrite? That is the report AirRenda is built to produce.

The site version of this comparison is https://airrenda.com/en/vs/airrenda-vs-alltherooms.

## Quick verdict

Choose **AirRenda** when you need a practical report on a specific property or a short list.

Choose **AllTheRooms** when you need enterprise-style STR intelligence, wide coverage, or portfolio-scale analysis and you have people who will actually use that depth.

## At a glance

| Need | AirRenda | AllTheRooms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research scale | One address or a working shortlist | Market, region, or portfolio |
| Experience | A single investor-oriented report | A data-rich platform for broader analytics |
| Best decision | Is this property worth deeper underwriting? | What is happening across many markets at once? |
| Time to a first answer | Click or type an address, then read | Better suited to ongoing data work than to a one-off showing |
| Team shape | Buyer, analyst, agent, small manager | Teams that want extensive datasets rather than a lightweight address report |

Confirm AllTheRooms' current datasets and packaging on their site. This page is about the job, not a feature-by-feature inventory.

## Where AirRenda is the better fit

- A new listing appeared this morning and a client wants an Airbnb read before the weekend.
- You are ranking ten addresses and you need every row to contain the same signals: score band, density, listed rates, competition, regulation exposure.
- The reader of the output is a buyer or an owner, not a data analyst.
- You want to spend credits on decisions, not on exploring a schema.

That is most of the Agency plan's world as well. Higher report volume is still address screening. It does not turn AirRenda into a market-wide feed.

## Where AllTheRooms may still win

- You are studying supply shifts across many cities, not touring a single building.
- Portfolio strategy depends on datasets a one-address report will never contain.
- The team already has analysts and a stack, and the missing piece is breadth.

AirRenda is not trying to replace that work. A fund can use a wide dataset to choose hunting grounds and AirRenda to accept or reject the buildings inside them.

## For small investors versus portfolio teams

Small investors usually get more from AirRenda, because the decision in front of them is a property, not a market model.

Portfolio teams may still want AirRenda as the individual-address gate even if AllTheRooms, AirDNA, or an internal warehouse covers the map. The gate is cheap relative to a bad acquisition. The warehouse does not automatically answer "this condo, this month."

## Questions

**Is AirRenda an AllTheRooms alternative?**
For property-level screening, it can be. For enterprise or market-wide intelligence, AllTheRooms is the closer fit.

**Which is better for a small investor?**
AirRenda, when the task is a specific address or a short list.

**Which is better for a portfolio team?**
Broad intelligence tools for the wide view. AirRenda still helps rank the individual addresses before a memo.
